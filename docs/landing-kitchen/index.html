Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a kitchen remodel cost in Los Angeles? +

Scope, layout changes, selections, and existing conditions all influence cost. A free consultation is the best starting point for a conversation grounded in your kitchen and priorities rather than a generic online number.

Can you help if I only have inspiration photos or a rough idea? +

Yes. We begin with the way you use the kitchen and the outcome you want. 3D visualization is part of the process to help make the design direction more concrete before construction.

Will I need permits for a kitchen remodel? +

Requirements depend on the work and the property’s jurisdiction. Los Angeles identifies kitchen remodeling, as well as new, expanded, or replaced electrical, mechanical, and plumbing work, among activities generally requiring permits; the City also notes some simpler projects may qualify for an Express Permit.

How long will my kitchen remodel take? +

The answer depends on the scope, required approvals, selected materials, and existing conditions. We discuss the planning sequence and project-specific factors before you make decisions based on timing.
