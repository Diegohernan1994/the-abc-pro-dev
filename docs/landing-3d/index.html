Frequently Asked Questions

I only have inspiration photos. Is it too early to contact you? +

No. Inspiration is a useful starting point, especially when paired with an explanation of what you need the room or home to do differently. The purpose of the initial conversation is to help define direction—not to require that every decision already be made.

Can you create blueprints or stamped architectural plans? +

Bring your needs or any existing plans to the consultation so the appropriate next step can be discussed.

Do I need a permit before I start planning? +

You do not need to resolve every permit question before the first conversation, but it should be part of responsible planning. The City of Los Angeles says requirements vary by project complexity: some work may qualify for an Express Permit, while more complex projects may require plan check and supporting documentation.

Why use 3D visualization? +

3D visualization provides a clearer preview of the project direction. It can make layout and style decisions easier to discuss before construction begins.
