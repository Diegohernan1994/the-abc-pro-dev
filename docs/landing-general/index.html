Frequently Asked Questions

What types of projects can I discuss in a general remodeling consultation? +

You can start with one space or a list of connected priorities. Tell us what is no longer working in your home and what you want to improve; the consultation is intended to help define a useful project direction.

How do I avoid misunderstanding the scope or cost of a remodel? +

Ask for clarity in writing before you commit. The CSLB says a home-improvement contract should describe the work and materials in detail, include a payment schedule, state who will secure permits, identify a completion date, and document approved changes through written change orders.

Do all general remodeling projects need permits? +

It depends on the proposed work and the property’s jurisdiction. LA County lists remodels and renovations, along with new or expanded electrical, mechanical, and plumbing work, among work generally requiring permits.

Can you help if I want to renovate in phases? +

Bring that priority to the conversation. The first step is to understand which spaces matter most and what should be considered together before work is planned.
