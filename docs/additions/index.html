That means your early decisions should account for the space you need, how the existing home works, the property location, and the approvals or documents the project may require.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a home addition cost in Los Angeles? +

Costs depend on the project scope, the existing home, the property, required documents or approvals, and the decisions made as the project is defined. A free conversation is the appropriate first step for discussing your objective and next steps; it is not a substitute for a project-specific scope and written agreement.

Do I need plans or blueprints before I contact you? +

No. Bring what you have, whether that is inspiration, a rough idea, or existing documents. The first conversation should help clarify what information is needed to move forward thoughtfully.

Will my home addition need permits? +

Additions generally require permits. LA County specifically lists additions among work that generally requires a permit, while the City of Los Angeles explains that the review path can depend on complexity and scope.

How long will approvals and construction take? +

Approval and construction timing depend on the property, scope, documents, municipal review, selections, and site conditions. We do not promise an approval date or a universal construction schedule.
